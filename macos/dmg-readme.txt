TDPlay AI Video Render
=====================

1. Drag "TDPlay AI Video Render" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications, Launchpad or Spotlight.

The first time, macOS may say it cannot check the app for malicious software
(the app is free and not registered with Apple). To open it anyway:

  1. Click "Done".
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to "TDPlay AI Video Render was blocked" and click "Open Anyway".
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only need to do this once.

On its first launch the app sets itself up: it downloads Python, Apple's MLX,
the LTX-2 code and ffmpeg (about 1 GB, a few minutes) into the TDPlay folder in
your home folder. Then it opens, and its Models tab downloads the video model.

Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or newer, 16 GB of
memory or more (32 GB recommended) and about 50 GB of free disk space.

Uninstall: drag the app to the Trash and delete the TDPlay folder in your home
folder (it also holds the models and your videos).
