TDPlay AI Video Render makes one continuous AI video shot, segment by segment, with picture and sound,
on your own PC. It is free and runs offline once it is set up.

What you need
- Windows 11 with a current AMD Adrenalin driver
- AMD Radeon RX 7900 / 7800 / 7700 / 7600 or RX 9070 / 9060 (or Radeon AI PRO R9700), or an NVIDIA RTX card
- 16 GB of RAM or more (32 GB recommended)
- About 70 GB of free disk space (an SSD helps)

What happens during the installation
After copying the app, a setup window finds your graphics card and downloads what the app needs:
Python, PyTorch for your card (AMD ROCm or NVIDIA CUDA), Lightricks' LTX-2 code and ffmpeg.
That is several GB and takes a while. At the end it offers to download the video model (about 55 GB);
you can also do that later in the app's Models tab.
